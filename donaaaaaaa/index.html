About

Mission

At Chika’s & Tea Café, we create more than just food and drinks—we create moments worth remembering. Our mission is to serve comforting flavors, refreshing teas, and carefully crafted coffee while providing a cozy space where laughter flows, stories are shared, and every customer feels at home.

Vision

To be a café where every cup comes with a story, every meal brings people closer, and every visit becomes a memory. Chika’s & Tea Café envisions becoming a beloved gathering place where good food, good tea, and good chika come together.
